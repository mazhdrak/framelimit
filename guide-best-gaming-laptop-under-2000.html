Why This Bracket Matters

A price ceiling is only useful when the exact configuration qualifies at checkout. Product-family names can cover different GPUs, displays, processors, memory, and power limits, so this guide distinguishes exact sourced configurations from related-model and specifications-only alternatives.

The Helios and Katana have exact-configuration game rows. The Legion's retail specifications are exact but its embedded performance data belongs to another model; TUF and Omen entries currently provide configuration context rather than comparable editorial evidence.
