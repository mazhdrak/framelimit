Methodology and Limitations

What is a reference price?

An editorial typical, street, or last-observed value stored with the exact catalog configuration. It is not a current Amazon price and is never placed in Product offer structured data.

When will monthly change appear?

The daily Amazon workflow archives one latest snapshot per calendar month. Month-over-month change appears only after two eligible live snapshots contain the same exact ASIN and that ASIN still matches the current catalog configuration.

How are missing prices handled?

They remain “Check retailer” rather than receiving an estimate from another configuration. Regional models, seller upgrades, storage, memory, and display options can materially change price.

What does this report represent?

A curated FRAMELIMIT catalog focused on current buyable gaming laptops, not a statistically complete census of every US listing.
