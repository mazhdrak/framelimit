This bracket contains several RTX 5060 configurations, but GPU power, display, memory, and live pricing vary substantially. The exact sourced benchmark rows below are the safer basis for comparing native, DLSS, and Frame Generation performance than a universal FPS claim.

Should You Spend More?

At $1,539, the ASUS TUF A18 brings the same RTX 5060 in an 18-inch chassis with more thermal headroom and a larger display — worth it if screen size matters. At $1,669, the HP Omen 16 Slim steps up to RTX 5070 — roughly 30% faster GPU for $320 more. The under $2,000 guide covers that jump in detail.

The key question is the live price gap. The LOQ 15 and TUF A16 both use 115W RTX 5060 configurations, while the Gigabyte CVH is capped at 85W. Compare their exact sourced benchmark rows, display, battery capacity, seller, and warranty before paying more for any one chassis.
