RAM and SSD Upgrade Guide

The 83F50053US starts with 32GB DDR5-6400 and a 1TB PCIe 5.0 NVMe SSD. Lenovo's 16IAX10H platform specification lists two DDR5-6400 CSODIMM slots with configurations offered up to 64GB. CSODIMM is an important compatibility detail: do not assume every conventional DDR5 SO-DIMM kit is an equivalent replacement.

ComponentVerified platform pathPractical recommendation
Memory2x DDR5 CSODIMM slots; DDR5-6400; up to 64GB Lenovo offeringKeep the matched 32GB configuration for gaming. For 64GB, use a 2x32GB CSODIMM kit explicitly qualified for the Legion Pro 7 16IAX10H.
StorageOne M.2 2280 PCIe 5.0 x4 slot and one M.2 2280 PCIe 4.0 x4 slotThe simplest capacity upgrade is a second PCIe 4.0 M.2 drive; retain the factory Gen 5 system drive unless replacement capacity is required.

See the laptop DDR5 guide for the SO-DIMM/CSODIMM distinction and verify the latest details in Lenovo's official PSREF.

Choose one matched 2x32GB kit and replace both factory modules. The DDR5-5600 SO-DIMM option is the lower-cost path and is expected to run at up to 5600 MT/s; the Crucial CT2K32G64C52CS5 CSODIMM search targets the 2x32GB DDR5-6400 kit. Do not mix SO-DIMM and CSODIMM modules, and confirm the exact part number before purchase.

Battery Life

The 99.9Wh battery is close to the usual carry-on limit, but actual runtime varies substantially with GPU mode, brightness, refresh rate, and workload. Treat it as capacity evidence rather than a universal runtime promise.

Build Quality & Ports

The published specification identifies an aluminium-and-magnesium chassis, RGB keyboard, Thunderbolt 4, USB-C charging support, HDMI 2.1, Ethernet, and a 1080p webcam with privacy shutter. Chassis rigidity, key feel, and tactile feedback are not presented as FRAMELIMIT hands-on observations.
