The measured 15IRX10 uses a 15-inch 1920x1200 IPS panel at 165Hz. Other Legion 5i Gen 10 listings can use different displays, so the full model code must be checked before purchase.

Battery Life

The 80Wh battery delivers 4–5 hours of productivity. Gaming drains it in about 70 minutes. Adequate but not a strength — the Legion 5i is a desk machine. The 300W power brick is large.
