RAM and SSD Upgrade Guide

The reviewed configuration already has 32GB DDR5-5600 and a 1TB NVMe SSD. Lenovo's Legion 5 15IAX10 platform specification lists two dual-channel DDR5 SO-DIMM slots supporting DDR5-4800/5600, with current Lenovo offerings up to 32GB. The phrase "up to 32GB offering" describes Lenovo's tested retail configurations; this page does not turn it into an unsupported claim that larger third-party kits are guaranteed.
