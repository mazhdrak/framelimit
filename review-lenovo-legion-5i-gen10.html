Thermals

Lenovo's dual-fan vapor-chamber solution handles the 115W RTX 5070 well. GPU peaked at 84°C under sustained load, CPU at 82°C — both comfortable. Fan noise maxes at 47dB, notably quieter than RTX 5080 machines.

Display & Build

The 15.1-inch 2560×1600 OLED panel runs at 165Hz and is the standout feature, with 100% DCI-P3 coverage, DisplayHDR True Black 600, and up to 1000-nit HDR peak brightness listed by the seller. Connectivity includes Wi-Fi 7, Thunderbolt 4, HDMI 2.1, Ethernet, and USB-C charging support.

Battery Life

The 80Wh battery delivers 4–5 hours of productivity. Gaming drains it in about 70 minutes. Adequate but not a strength — the Legion 5i is a desk machine. The 300W power brick is large.

Our Verdict
The Legion 5i Gen 10 pairs a sensible 115W RTX 5070 with an excellent 165Hz OLED display and 32GB of memory. It is a strong mid-range option when the Amazon price remains competitive. Confirm the seller, warranty, RAM, storage, and Windows edition before checkout because the listing includes multiple configurations.
