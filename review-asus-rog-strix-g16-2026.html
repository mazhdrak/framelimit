ASUS ROG Strix G16 (2026) Review

The 2026 Strix G16 is ASUS' performance-first 16-inch model below the SCAR line. Its headline configuration combines a 175W RTX 5080, upgradeable DDR5 and a 240Hz Mini LED panel with more than 2,000 dimming zones.

Verdict

On paper this is one of the most complete RTX 5080 configurations: full GPU power, replaceable memory and a serious HDR display. It remains unranked until independent retail-unit results establish thermals, acoustics and pricing. Buyers should also verify the exact G615 suffix because panel and GPU options vary.

Pros
  • Full 175W RTX 5080 ceiling
  • Upgradeable memory and storage
  • 240Hz Mini LED with strong HDR hardware
  • 2.5GbE and practical rear/side port layout
Cons
  • Heavy for a 16-inch laptop
  • Retail configurations vary by region
  • No verified direct Amazon ASIN yet
  • Independent benchmark coverage is still limited
