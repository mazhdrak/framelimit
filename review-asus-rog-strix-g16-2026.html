Verdict

This G615LW is the performance-first alternative to the Zephyrus G16. The 175W RTX 5080, upgradeable DDR5 and large cooling system suit long gaming sessions, while the 2.65kg chassis is firmly desktop-replacement territory.

Pros
  • Full 175W RTX 5080 power
  • 64GB memory in the linked configuration
  • 240Hz 2560×1600 Nebula display
  • Upgradeable memory and storage
Cons
  • Heavy and bulky
  • IPS rather than OLED or Mini LED
  • Retailer-upgraded 64GB bundle
