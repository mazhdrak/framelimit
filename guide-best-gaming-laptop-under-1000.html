Why There Is No Permanent Pick

Sub-$1,000 inventory is promotion-driven, and one family name can cover different CPUs, GPUs, power limits, displays, memory, and storage. A launch price, old sale, used listing, or family-level specification does not prove that a specific new configuration qualifies today.

FRAMELIMIT therefore publishes no numbered winner here without a fresh eligible offer and configuration-matched evidence. If no candidate passes both tests, use the under-$1,500 guide for exact RTX 5060 configurations rather than treating an old discount as current.

Bottom Line Verify the exact SKU before comparing value. Prioritise the requirements that affect your workload—measured performance, display, memory, storage, cooling evidence, serviceability, seller, warranty, and return terms.

Deal Traps To Check

Do not infer performance from an “RTX” badge or GPU tier alone. Confirm the GPU power limit and matching measurements. Treat missing memory layout, storage slots, panel brightness or colour coverage, seller identity, warranty, and return terms as unresolved fields—not as favourable assumptions.
