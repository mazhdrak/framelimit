Lenovo Legion 5 15AHP10 32GB Review

The current retail configuration combines Ryzen 7 260, RTX 5060 graphics, 32GB upgradeable DDR5 and a 165Hz OLED panel.

Verdict

The 15AHP10 is a balanced current-generation gaming laptop: its OLED display is the standout feature, while the RTX 5060 is best suited to 1080p or optimized 1600p gaming. The 32GB retail configuration avoids the immediate memory upgrade required by cheaper base models.

Pros
  • Sharp 165Hz OLED display
  • 32GB upgradeable DDR5
  • Current RTX 5060 platform
  • Good serviceability
Cons
  • RTX 5060 has 8GB VRAM
  • Can overlap faster RTX 5070 laptops on price
  • Glossy OLED needs care in bright rooms
  • Third-party upgraded retail configuration
