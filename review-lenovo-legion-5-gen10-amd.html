OLED or More GPU Power?

The central trade-off is a 165Hz OLED screen paired with an RTX 5060. Buy this configuration because you want that display and its overall size, not because OLED makes the graphics processor faster. Its 2560×1600 native resolution is a different workload from 1080p; evaluate demanding games at the settings you will actually use. Upscaling can be an option, but this page does not promise a particular frame rate or visual result for an untested title.

If the current total approaches an RTX 5070 alternative, compare the screen and complete configuration alongside game-specific evidence. A higher GPU tier is not automatically the better purchase for someone who mainly plays lighter games and values the built-in display. Conversely, a buyer chasing demanding native-resolution performance should not pay an OLED premium without checking the relevant benchmarks.

Planning a Memory or SSD Upgrade

The linked offer has 16GB of upgradeable DDR5 and a 1TB SSD. Check whether the seller supplies one or two memory modules before ordering an upgrade. Capacity and channel layout are different questions; the family name cannot answer either. Follow Lenovo's exact service instructions and verify module compatibility rather than transferring an upgrade limit from a Legion 5i or Legion Pro.

Include any necessary upgrade in your comparison price. An apparently cheaper 16GB offer can be less attractive if you already know your recording, editing or modded-game workload needs more memory. If 16GB is enough for your current use, keeping the factory setup avoids unnecessary spending. For storage, establish drive occupancy and supported dimensions before assuming a second SSD can be added without replacing the first.

Using It Away from a Desk

The listed 80Wh battery and approximately 1.9kg chassis are useful planning figures, but runtime and total bag weight need more context. Add the charger and accessories you carry. For classroom or office use, check brightness, reflections, keyboard comfort and fan behavior in a quiet environment. The glossy display can make placement near a window more important than a peak-brightness specification suggests.
