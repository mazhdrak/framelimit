Why FRAMELIMIT Exists

Gaming laptop specifications are fragmented across manufacturer pages, retailer listings, and reviews that often test different configurations. That makes it difficult to decide whether a laptop is worth your money.

FRAMELIMIT exists to organize that evidence. Reviews separate official specifications, retailer information, externally published test results, and FRAMELIMIT editorial analysis. We do not present sourced results as measurements from our own lab.

I also care about TGP accuracy — the single most important number that laptop reviewers and retailers routinely obscure. A laptop sold as "RTX 5080" can range from 80W to 175W. That's the difference between desktop-class performance and a machine that struggles at 1080p. I always list the actual TGP in every review and guide.

How I Evaluate

🎮
Benchmark Comparison
We compare published results for Cyberpunk 2077, Black Myth: Wukong, Alan Wake 2, Forza Horizon 5, Elden Ring, Indiana Jones, Red Dead Redemption 2, Call of Duty: Black Ops 6, Spider-Man 2, and Baldur's Gate 3. Results are normalized by resolution, preset, power mode, and upscaling settings.
🌡️
Sustained Performance
We prioritize published tests that include sustained gaming or stress loads. Short peak results are not treated as proof of long-term performance, and incompatible test conditions are not combined.
🔋
Battery Evidence
Battery results are compared only when the workload, brightness, power mode, and configuration are documented. Capacity and charging specifications come from manufacturer documentation.
📊
Synthetic Evidence
Published 3DMark and Cinebench results help compare systems when the exact configuration and test conditions match. Their source remains separate from FRAMELIMIT editorial conclusions.

Editorial Independence

FRAMELIMIT earns revenue through affiliate commissions (primarily Amazon Associates). When you buy through our links, we earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how independent review sites stay independent — we're not beholden to any brand's advertising budget.

This revenue model means our incentive is to give you accurate recommendations, not to push specific products. If I recommend a laptop and you buy it and hate it, you won't trust this site again. That accountability is what keeps the reviews honest.
