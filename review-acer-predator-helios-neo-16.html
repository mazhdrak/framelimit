The exact PHN16S-71-98RF test unit uses a 16-inch 2560×1600 OLED panel at 240Hz. At 2.18kg it is relatively portable for this hardware class, with upgradeable storage and memory.

Pros
  • Best thermals of any RTX 5070 laptop at this price

Our Verdict
The Acer Predator Helios Neo 16 AI is a strong option for buyers who prioritize RTX 5070 Ti performance, a 240Hz WQXGA display, and upgradeable components over portability. Its recommendation depends on the current checkout price and seller warranty.
