Lenovo Legion 7 Gen 11 Review

The Legion 7 Gen 11 is a departure from Lenovo's heavier performance-first designs. It combines an aluminium chassis below 1.8kg with an excellent 240Hz OLED, Ryzen AI 400 processing and a 115W RTX 5060. It is closer to a creator-friendly portable gaming machine than a Legion Pro replacement.

Verdict

Independent testing of the Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 and RTX 5060 configuration confirms strong 1080p performance for a sub-1.8kg laptop. The GPU performs slightly above the broader RTX 5060 Laptop average in synthetic tests, although its 115W ceiling and 8GB VRAM still place it below RTX 5070-class alternatives for demanding native-resolution gaming.

Pros
  • Under 1.8kg aluminium chassis
  • Excellent 240Hz OLED specification
  • Two USB4 ports and an SD reader
  • 84Wh battery with strong official productivity claims
Cons
  • RTX 5060 is the only listed discrete GPU
  • Memory is soldered
  • No onboard Ethernet
  • Independent thermals and fan-noise results still limited
