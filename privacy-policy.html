Information We Collect

FRAMELIMIT is a content and affiliate website. Cookies and Affiliate Links

Some outbound product links are affiliate links. When you click them, the retailer may set cookies or use similar technologies to attribute a purchase. FRAMELIMIT may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate relationships do not influence our scores, rankings, or editorial conclusions.

Analytics

We may use analytics to understand which pages are useful, fix technical issues, and improve buying guides. Analytics data is used in aggregate and is not sold.

Advertising and Retail Partners

Retailers and affiliate networks may process your data under their own privacy policies after you leave FRAMELIMIT. Review the privacy policy of any retailer before purchasing.

Your Choices

You can block cookies in your browser settings. You can also avoid affiliate tracking by navigating directly to a retailer instead of clicking a product link on this site.
