Gaming Performance

Display — 16:10 at This Price Is Rare

The 1920×1200 16:10 IPS panel at 165Hz is a useful differentiator in the budget tier. The extra vertical pixels matter for productivity tasks, and 165Hz is more than adequate for 1080p gaming. Published measurements should be compared only against panels tested with a matching protocol.

Battery & Build

The measured configuration uses a 76Wh battery. Runtime varies by graphics mode, brightness, and workload, so it should not be compared with another model unless the test protocol is matched.

Ports cover the basics: three USB-A ports, one USB-C port with DisplayPort 1.4 and Power Delivery 3.0, HDMI 2.1, Gigabit Ethernet, and a 3.5mm audio jack. There is no SD card reader.

Our Verdict
The Gigabyte Gaming A16 CVH is a credible lower-power RTX 5060 option with a 16:10 165Hz display, upgradeable memory and storage, and a 76Wh battery. Compare the live price with 115W RTX 5060 alternatives, and verify the exact model code, seller, warranty, RAM, and storage before checkout.
