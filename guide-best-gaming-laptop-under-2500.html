Frequently Asked Questions

Can you get an RTX 5080 gaming laptop under $2,500?

Not at our October 19, 2026 check. None of the RTX 5080 configurations we track had a qualifying new offer at or under $2,500 on Amazon US. The closest options are in our under-$3,000 guide. Under this budget, the strongest GPU we confirmed is the RTX 5070 Ti in the MSI Vector 16 HX AI.

RTX 5070 Ti or RTX 5070: which is better under $2,500?

The RTX 5070 Ti has 12GB of video memory against 8GB on the RTX 5070, which matters more as texture settings and resolution go up. In this guide, though, the RTX 5070 Ti Vector comes with 16GB RAM, a 512GB SSD and a basic FHD+ 144Hz screen, while the RTX 5070 Katana 15 HX has 32GB RAM, 1TB and a 1440p 165Hz panel. Choose the Vector if the GPU comes first and you plan to upgrade storage; choose the Katana for a more complete laptop out of the box.

What GPU power (TGP) should I look for at this price?

For an RTX 5070, 115W is the top of the laptop range, and the Katana 15 HX is configured at up to 115W. Smaller designs such as the ROG Zephyrus G14 run the same GPU at up to 90W to fit a thinner chassis. The GPU name alone does not tell you sustained performance, so check the configured power for the exact model before buying.

Is the ROG Flow Z13 a real gaming laptop?

It is a 13.4-inch tablet with a detachable keyboard. Its Radeon 8060S graphics are built into the processor and share system memory, so it is a portable all-rounder rather than a replacement for an RTX 5070 laptop. Read the review before choosing it on gaming performance alone.
