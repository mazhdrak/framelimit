Verdict

The Raider 18 is built for buyers who want desktop-level GPU power, a large HDR display and upgradeable components. Its 3.6kg chassis and 400W-class power supply make it a transportable workstation rather than a travel laptop.

Pros
  • 175W RTX 5090 with 24GB VRAM
  • 64GB upgradeable DDR5
  • 4K 120Hz Mini LED HDR display
  • Thunderbolt 5 and dual M.2 support
Cons
  • Very heavy
  • Expensive
  • Short gaming battery life
  • Large power adapter
