FRAMELIMIT VERDICT — 9.3 / 10

The MSI Raider 18 HX AI is the premium 18-inch pick for buyers who want a large display, full TGP, and maximum upgradeability. At $3,543 it matches the Razer Blade 16's price but offers an 18-inch screen and user-serviceable internals. The trade-off is weight (3.1kg) and no OLED — but as a desktop replacement that rarely moves, those are acceptable compromises.

The 18-Inch Advantage

The 18-inch 2560×1600 panel at 240Hz offers more physical screen area than 16-inch machines. Published panel measurements indicate good brightness and wide color coverage, though results vary by unit and profile. The PCIe Gen 5 NVMe configuration also provides high storage bandwidth; verify the installed drive because retailer configurations can differ.

Upgradeability

The Raider 18 is the most upgradeable flagship laptop available. Both RAM slots are accessible (supporting up to 64GB), two M.2 slots for NVMe drives, and a straightforward service panel. If you plan to keep this machine 3+ years and upgrade along the way, the Raider 18 is the only flagship that supports that plan.

Our Verdict
The MSI Raider 18 HX AI is for the buyer who games at a desk, wants the larger screen experience, and values upgradeability over thinness. At $3,543 — the same as the Razer Blade 16 — you're choosing between OLED + portability (Blade) or screen size + upgradeability + quieter fans (Raider). Both are excellent machines for different users. If the laptop never leaves your desk, the Raider 18 is the smarter long-term buy.
