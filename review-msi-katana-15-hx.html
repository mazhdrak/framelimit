RTX 5070 vs RTX 5060 — Is It Worth the Jump?

The Katana 15 HX moves up to a 115W RTX 5070 and offers an optional 2560×1440 panel, while the Gigabyte Gaming A16 sits in the RTX 5060 tier. The practical value gap depends on the exact game, settings, seller, and current prices; a universal percentage uplift is not claimed without a matching test protocol.

Memory and SSD Planning

MSI's B14WGK-298NZ sheet lists two memory slots and a maximum 96GB capacity, but only one M.2 storage slot. These are regional specifications: verify the full seller model before applying them. Do not buy a second internal drive on the assumption that there is a free slot.

If your exact machine also has a single occupied M.2 slot, expanding internal capacity means replacing the existing drive and planning a backup, migration or fresh installation. Include any enclosure or installation cost when comparing a larger factory SSD offer. For RAM, confirm module compatibility and the existing arrangement before buying a kit.

Is 8GB VRAM Suitable for Your Games?

Choose a demanding game you expect to play and inspect results at your intended settings. Texture detail, ray tracing and rendering resolution can change the memory requirement. Lowering one of those settings may be preferable to paying for a different laptop, but decide using image quality and frame-time consistency rather than a headline FPS number alone.

System RAM and graphics memory are separate. Installing more DDR5 does not increase the GPU's 8GB capacity. Likewise, generated frames do not establish the responsiveness of a higher native frame rate. In the linked benchmark table, read the preset and rendering-mode columns together before comparing the numbers.

Choosing Between This Katana and a Cheaper Laptop

Compare the exact offer with a configuration that already meets your games and work requirements. If a lower-priced option provides acceptable performance, the saving may matter more than an unused specification advantage. If you need the Katana's listed QHD display and memory capacity, compare the cost of obtaining those features on the alternative instead of comparing GPU names alone.

For daily travel, include the adapter in your weight comparison and look for a battery test matching your workload. At a desk, list the monitor and peripheral connections you actually need. Confirm the retailer's return terms before relying on a panel, keyboard or noise characteristic that you have not tried.
