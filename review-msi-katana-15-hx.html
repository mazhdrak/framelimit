Battery & Build

The Katana uses a 75Wh battery. The chassis is plastic and weighs about 2.4kg, so it is movable but not a thin-and-light design. Ports include three USB-A connections, USB-C, HDMI 2.1, Ethernet, and a headphone jack; there is no card reader.
