How FRAMELIMIT verifies an upgrade

  1. Identify the full machine type or retail model code, not only the marketing family.
  2. Check the manufacturer's platform specification and hardware maintenance documentation.
  3. Separate the factory configuration from the platform limit. "Offered with 32GB" does not always mean "electrically limited to 32GB."
  4. Match module format, generation, speed, voltage, capacity, rank, and BIOS support before recommending a kit.
  5. For SSDs, verify M.2 length, PCIe generation, keying, available slot, heatsink clearance, and whether the second drive needs a mounting screw or thermal pad.

Before opening the laptop

Back up important files, shut down fully, disconnect the charger, follow the manufacturer's maintenance manual, and disconnect the battery when the documented procedure requires it. Opening a bottom panel can affect warranty handling in some regions, and accidental damage is generally not covered. If you are unsure, use an authorized service provider.

FRAMELIMIT verifies compatibility from manufacturer documentation. Product availability and specifications can change.
