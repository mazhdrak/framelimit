MSI Raider 16 Max HX Review

MSI compresses its desktop-replacement formula into a 16-inch chassis here: up to 300W combined CPU/GPU power, a 175W RTX 5090, two upgradeable memory slots and both Gen4 and Gen5 storage. The 240Hz OLED is the major upgrade over older Raider displays.

Verdict

Independent reviews agree that performance and display quality are the headline strengths. The compromise is equally clear: 2.6kg plus a 400W adapter, mixed chassis impressions and high fan output. It is a mobile workstation in size 16 clothing, not a thin flagship.

Pros
  • Full 175W RTX 5090 option
  • Two RAM slots with support up to 128GB
  • Excellent 240Hz OLED specification
  • 2.5GbE, SD Express and broad port selection
Cons
  • 2.6kg plus a large 400W adapter
  • Build quality is less premium than the price suggests
  • High sustained fan noise is expected
  • Exact RTX 5090 retail configurations remain scarce
