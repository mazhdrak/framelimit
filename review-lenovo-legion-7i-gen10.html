FRAMELIMIT VERDICT — 9.0 / 10

The Legion 7i Gen 10 pairs a Core Ultra 9 275HX and a 115W RTX 5070 with a 16-inch OLED in an aluminium chassis under 2kg. The 9.0 is a historical editorial assessment, not an exact-unit measurement or a current-price value ranking. The main limits are the 8GB RTX 5070, which cannot be upgraded to a higher GPU tier in this chassis, and fan noise in Performance mode.

Ultrabookreview reports no thermal throttling in its 3DMark stress loop, flat on a desk or on a stand, and calls the laptop well cooled but loud in Performance mode. Custom mode added only about 2–3% to frame rates over Performance. Sustained CPU power is about 95W in Performance mode, so the 275HX scores roughly 7–8% below the same processor in the Legion Pro 7i. With the laptop flat on a desk, CPU temperatures in the CPU test reached the low to mid 90s °C.

Display

Lenovo lists the 240Hz panel as a glossy 16-inch 2560×1600 OLED with 500 nits typical and 1,100 nits peak brightness, 100% DCI-P3, DisplayHDR True Black 1000 and G-SYNC. Ultrabookreview's 165Hz OLED reached close to 500 nits of sustained SDR brightness and uses high-frequency PWM at all brightness levels. Its measurements belong to the 165Hz panel and are not transferred to the 240Hz panel. Glossy OLED reflects more in bright rooms than a matte IPS panel.

Battery

The battery is 84Wh. Ultrabookreview measured roughly 4–6 hours of light use (text editing, video, browsing) at about 120 nits and 60Hz. It did not publish a gaming runtime, so none is quoted here. The laptop also accepts USB-C charging.
