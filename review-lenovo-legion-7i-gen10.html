Display

The Legion 7i shares the Pro 7i's 16" 2560×1600 OLED panel at 240Hz — a display specification also found in higher GPU tiers. Infinite contrast ratio, true blacks, 100% DCI-P3 coverage, and a 0.2ms response time. At 400 nits peak brightness it's not as punchy as an IPS panel in a bright room, but for gaming in controlled lighting it's exceptional. The 240Hz refresh rate is noticeably smoother than the Legion 5i's 165Hz, and at RTX 5070 performance levels you can consistently hit 200+ fps in competitive titles to fully exploit it.
