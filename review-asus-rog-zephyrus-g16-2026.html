Verdict

The GU605CW is the portability-first RTX 5080 choice. Its 1.85kg aluminium chassis and 240Hz OLED are exceptional for travel and creative work, while the 120W GPU ceiling means thicker 175W laptops remain faster in sustained gaming.

Pros
  • Excellent 240Hz OLED display
  • RTX 5080 performance below 2kg
  • 32GB memory and 2TB storage
  • Strong creator-focused port selection
Cons
  • 120W GPU ceiling trails full-power flagships
  • Soldered memory
  • Premium retailer pricing
  • Retailer-upgraded SKU rather than a base ASUS package
