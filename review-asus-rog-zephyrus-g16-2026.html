Verdict

This is the balanced choice for buyers who split time between gaming, creative work and travel. Native-resolution gaming performance is a few frames behind thicker 175W systems, but the 1.95kg weight, OLED panel and unusually strong battery result make that compromise coherent.

Pros
  • 240Hz OLED with 100% DCI-P3 coverage
  • Only 1.95kg with RTX 5080 graphics
  • Excellent measured productivity battery life
  • Strong CPU performance and premium construction
Cons
  • Memory is soldered
  • Manual mode is required for the maximum GPU power target
  • Native QHD ray tracing often needs DLSS
  • Exact regional configurations vary substantially
