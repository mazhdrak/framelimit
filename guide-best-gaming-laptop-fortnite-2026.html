What Actually Matters for Fortnite on Laptop

Fortnite scales with both CPU and GPU. Performance mode reduces GPU load, while Epic quality makes the GPU more important. For competitive play, prioritize display refresh rate, stable 1% lows, CPU behavior, and the exact tested rendering mode. Do not infer a guaranteed frame rate from the GPU name alone.

Upscaling and Frame Generation can change displayed frame rate, image quality, and latency behavior, so native, Super Resolution, and generated-frame results must stay separate. A GPU family or feature label cannot substitute for a measured Fortnite replay on the exact laptop configuration.
