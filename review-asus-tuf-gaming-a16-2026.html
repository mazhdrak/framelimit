Verdict

The ASUS TUF Gaming A16 is a practical performance platform with a Ryzen 9 270, an RTX 5070 Laptop GPU rated up to 115W, two replaceable memory slots, two M.2 slots and a large 90Wh battery. Its 1920×1200 165Hz panel reduces the GPU load compared with a 2560×1600 screen, while G-Sync and Advanced Optimus support suit gaming use.

The linked 64GB configuration is a retailer modification. ASUS lists 32GB as two 16GB modules for the corresponding factory configuration and a 64GB platform maximum. FRAMELIMIT has not tested this unit, so this review does not score its gaming speed, thermals, noise, battery life or display quality.
