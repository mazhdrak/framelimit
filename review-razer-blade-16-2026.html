Razer Blade 16 (2026) Review

The 2026 Blade 16 corrects the biggest weakness of the previous generation: its CPU platform. Intel Panther Lake, faster LPDDR5X memory and a 165W RTX 5090 make this a materially stronger machine while preserving the slim CNC aluminium chassis and 240Hz OLED.

Verdict

The strongest argument for the Blade 16 is still the combination of flagship graphics, a premium chassis and travel-friendly weight. Independent testing confirms excellent gaming performance, but the fixed memory, premium price and lower GPU ceiling than bulky 175W systems remain important trade-offs.

Pros
  • Excellent 240Hz OLED with strong HDR specification
  • RTX 5090 in a 2.14kg CNC aluminium chassis
  • Thunderbolt 5 and broad creator connectivity
  • Much stronger CPU platform than the prior Blade 16
Cons
  • LPDDR5X memory is not upgradeable
  • 165W GPU trails the largest 175W desktop replacements
  • Premium pricing and no verified Amazon ASIN yet
  • Thin chassis requires more aggressive fan tuning under load
