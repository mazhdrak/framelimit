Alienware 16 Aurora RTX 5060 Review

The current replacement for the discontinued Dell G16 entry combines a Core 7 240H, RTX 5060 and 32GB DDR5.

Verdict

The 16 Aurora brings Alienware's design and support ecosystem into a more affordable RTX 5060 configuration. The 120Hz panel is sharp but slower than the 165Hz and 240Hz screens common at this price.
