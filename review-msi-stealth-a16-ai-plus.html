Thermals, Battery and Travel

The official US listing specifies a 99.9Wh battery and a 240W adapter. Neither capacity nor adapter rating establishes measured runtime. Compare unplugged use with the same screen brightness, graphics mode and workload; do not expect plugged-in gaming performance to carry over to battery power.

MSI's listing gives approximately 4.39lb in one section and 4.63lb in its detailed table. Budget around 2.0–2.1kg for the laptop, plus its charger, and confirm the shipping configuration if travel weight is decisive. We have not measured this exact unit's fan noise or sustained temperatures; look for sustained-load and battery tests of the matching configuration before relying on either for a purchase.

OLED Display and Desk Setup

The manufacturer lists a 2560×1600 OLED panel at 240Hz with 100% DCI-P3 coverage. Those specifications describe resolution, refresh rate and intended gamut; they do not prove calibration accuracy or measured brightness. For editing, verify a suitable profile and your color-managed workflow. For games, compare actual frame rates at 1600p rather than assuming that every title will reach 240fps.

The official configuration has HDMI 2.1, USB4 with DisplayPort and power delivery, two USB-A ports and 2.5Gb Ethernet, but no card reader. A photographer should allow for a USB reader; a dock user should check display support and charging limits. Use the included adapter for performance comparisons instead of assuming a USB-C dock supplies equivalent power.

Memory, Storage and Configuration Traps

MSI explicitly lists 32GB LPDDR5X and a 2TB Gen4 SSD for A3XWHG-079US. Amazon's generic DDR5 wording should not override the OEM memory specification. The benchmark unit's 64GB capacity is a different configuration, not evidence that this retail machine can be upgraded to match it.

Before ordering, match the full A3XWHG-079US code, 32GB memory and 2TB drive. For future SSD work, consult the exact service documentation for access, slot occupancy and clearance. Keeping the factory 2TB drive may be more practical than buying an upgrade immediately; large recording libraries can also use external storage, subject to the enclosure and port speed.
