What Matters for Video Editing — Gaming vs Dedicated Workstation

Video-editing performance depends on the application version, codec, media, effects, resolution, encoder path, memory, storage, drivers, power mode, and thermal state. Gaming specifications can make a laptop a credible candidate, but they do not prove export speed or timeline behaviour against a creator workstation.

Set VRAM, system-memory, storage, display, and CPU requirements from the projects you actually handle. For colour-critical work, require measured gamut, accuracy, brightness, HDR behaviour, calibration method, and output validation; an OLED or Mini-LED label alone is not sufficient.

Do You Actually Need OLED for Video Editing?

Panel technology alone does not determine suitability for editing. Compare measured gamut, accuracy, brightness, contrast, uniformity, HDR behaviour, calibration controls, surface finish, and output pipeline for the exact panel. Colour-critical or HDR delivery may still require a calibrated external reference display and a validated colour-managed workflow.
