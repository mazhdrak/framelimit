What Each Configuration Prioritizes

The LOQ is the performance-first option on paper: its RTX 5060 power ceiling is 30W higher and its exact dataset explicitly records that ceiling. The Nitro offers the stronger out-of-box capacity package: twice the memory and storage, a taller 16:10 180Hz panel, a larger 76Wh battery and a slightly lower listed weight.

Both memory configurations are upgradeable. The LOQ's smaller 512GB drive is likely to require an earlier storage expansion for large game libraries. The Nitro's 85W GPU limit can reduce graphics headroom relative to higher-power RTX 5060 designs, but exact sustained behavior remains chassis- and mode-dependent.
