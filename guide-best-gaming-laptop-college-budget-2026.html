What Actually Matters for a College Gaming Laptop

Students should compare battery capacity, weight, build durability, display workspace, and gaming performance separately. Advertised runtime is not directly comparable unless the workload, brightness, graphics mode, and test protocol match.

Every laptop on this list includes an RTX 5060, but the verified GPU power limits range from 85W to 115W. Use the exact sourced benchmark rows rather than assuming that every RTX 5060 laptop performs identically.
